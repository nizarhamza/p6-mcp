# Code of conduct

P6 MCP is used by planners, schedulers and engineers around the world. Everyone who takes part in this
project (issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and any other space of the project) helps keep it a
respectful and welcoming place, whatever their experience, background, identity or language.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with people new to P6, MCP or open source.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Disagree with facts and accept that others may see it differently.
- Give and accept constructive feedback; admit and correct your own mistakes.
- Remember that many people here write in a second language.
- Respect confidentiality: never post a real schedule, P6 database, XER file, log or screenshot that
  belongs to someone else or names real projects, clients or people. Use the synthetic test database
  (`tests/fixtures/make_db.py`) instead.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, trolling.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing private information of others (contact details, project data) without their explicit
  permission.
- Threats, or encouraging anyone to harm themselves or others.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report privately through GitHub:

- On an issue, a pull request or a comment: open its **...** menu, choose **Report content**, then
  **Report to repository admins**. Only the maintainer sees the report.
- For anything else, or when the problem involves the maintainer: use GitHub's
  [Report abuse](https://github.com/contact/report-abuse) form, which goes to GitHub.

Reports are handled promptly and confidentially, and the person who reports is protected from
retaliation.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or delete comments, commits, issues and pull requests, lock conversations
and block accounts that break this code. Depending on the impact:

1. **Correction**: a private note explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **Warning**: no further interaction with the people involved for a set time. Breaking it leads to a
   ban.
3. **Temporary ban** from any interaction with the project.
4. **Permanent ban**, for a pattern of violations, harassment of a person, or aggression toward a group.
