Primavera P6 with an AI assistant: guides

Each guide gives the prompts to type, the tools the assistant calls and real results from the public demo project DEMO-SS225, a 225/63 kV substation schedule with synthetic data.

  • DCMA 14-point check with AIRun the 14 checks with the thresholds set in P6, read each failed check, drill into the activities behind it and follow the trend between updates.
  • Driving path in P6 with ClaudeTrace what drives an activity back to its root cause, see where its total float comes from, then test a change in memory.
  • Comparing two P6 updates with AIActivities and links compared one by one, slip causes, float erosion, and the integrity flags that explain a finish date that moved.
  • AI review of an XER before submissionFile checks, logic, progress and integrity of an update XER, without importing it, before it goes to the client or after it arrives.
